COMBAT TIPS

      
► You must be in CLOSE RANGE to land punches & kicks

      
► Special 1 fires a RANGED PROJECTILE — works from mid range

      
► Hold BLOCK to absorb hits — takes only 12% damage

      
► POWER BAR fills as you fight — specials cost 40, super costs 100

      
► Land hits quickly to build COMBO CHAINS

      
► Win 2 rounds to claim victory. Best of 3.

    


    

      
DIFFICULTY LEVELS

      
NOVICE  Very slow AI, rarely blocks or uses specials

      
EASY  Slow reactions, basic attack patterns

      
MEDIUM  Balanced — good intro challenge

      
HARD  Fast reactions, frequent specials & blocks

      
BRUTAL  Aggressive, difficult to counter

      
INSANE  Near-instant reactions, dodges your attacks

      
MASTER  Maximum AI — reacts in ~50ms. Godlike.
